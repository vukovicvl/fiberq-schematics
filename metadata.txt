name=FiberQ Schematics
description=Design fiber-optic schematics with equipment, cables, splicing, tracing and optical budgets.
about=Create and edit independent fiber-optic schematic workspaces inside QGIS.
 Includes ODFs, patch panels, FDH/MDU cabinets, FAT/OTB boxes, closures,
 splitters, OLT/ONT equipment, cable catalogues, snapping, fiber operations,
 tracing, reports, optical budgets, legends and PDF/SVG/DXF exports.
 Uses separate GeoPackages and schematic EPSG:3857 positions, not geographic coordinates.
 Start in a blank QGIS project. No external FiberQ plugin, AutoCAD, online account
 or additional Python packages are required. English and Serbian UI are included.
